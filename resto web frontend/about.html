Our Story

Since 1999

Preserving the Art of
Traditional Cooking

Savora was born from a deep-rooted love for the diverse culinary traditions of India. Our journey began in the heart of South Delhi, with a simple goal: to serve food that honors our heritage while embracing modern refinement.

Every spice blend is toasted and ground in-house, every slow-cooked curry is watched with patience, and every tandoori dish is fired to perfection. We don't just serve meals; we offer a tribute to the royal kitchens of India.
