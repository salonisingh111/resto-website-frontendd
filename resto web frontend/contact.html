Common Queries

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer vegetarian and vegan options?

Yes, we have an extensive range of vegetarian dishes, and many can be prepared vegan upon request. Our kitchen uses separate utensils and preparation areas for vegetarian food.

Is there a dress code?

Our dress code is smart casual. We kindly ask our guests to avoid wearing athletic wear, beachwear, or flip-flops to maintain the premium dining atmosphere.

Do you have private dining rooms?

Yes, we have three private dining rooms that can accommodate between 8 to 24 guests. Perfect for business meetings, intimate celebrations, and family gatherings.

Is parking available?

We offer complimentary valet parking for all our dining guests. Simply pull up to the main entrance and our staff will assist you.
